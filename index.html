2. Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

A. Types of Data Collected

Account Personal Data

We may collect the following information when You create or sign in to Your Account:

  • Email address

  • Display Name (if provided via Google sign-in)

  • Firebase User Identifier (UID)

Detection and History Metadata

If You activate the sound detection service and are logged in, We store the following metadata in Your Detection History on Firebase Firestore:

  • Sound Labels: The specific type of sound classified by the AI (e.g., "Siren", "Fire Alarm").

  • Maximum Score: The confidence level for each detected sound.

  • Timestamps: The start time, last seen time, and creation time of the detection session.

Crucially, for the passive sound detection feature, We DO NOT record or store the raw audio data on Our servers.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically and may include Your device's IP address, device type, mobile operating system, and other diagnostic data.

B. Use of High-Risk Permissions (Compliance with Google Play)

Our Application requires the following sensitive permissions to deliver its core assistive functions. The use of these permissions is strictly limited and requires Your explicit consent upon first use.

1. Microphone Access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

  • Primary Use (Alarm Feature): Audio is captured continuously via a **Foreground Service** to enable **Assistive Audio AI** processing (YAMNet model) on the device. The raw audio is processed instantly and **discarded**; it is never recorded or stored by the Company for this purpose.
  • Secondary Use (On-Demand): If You explicitly choose to use the **"Start Transcription"** or **"Recognize Music"** features, the audio is temporarily captured and transmitted to third-party providers (Deepgram and AudD) for analysis, solely to fulfill Your request.

2. Display Over Other Apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

  • Purpose: This permission is used **ONLY** to display the mandatory **Full-Screen Alarm Alert** over the lock screen or other running applications when a critical sound is detected in the background, ensuring You receive the safety notification.

3. Camera Access (Flashlight)

  • Purpose: Access to the Camera hardware is used **EXCLUSIVELY** to control the device's **Flashlight** as a visual alarm effect, if configured by You in the Alarm Settings. We do not use this permission to record images or video.

C. Sharing of Your Personal Data with Third-Party Providers

We share Your data with the following Third-Party Providers to enable the functionality of the Service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore): Used for Account management, storing User Settings, and storing **Detection History Metadata**.

  • Deepgram: When You activate the **"Start Transcription"** feature, **real-time audio** is streamed to Deepgram for speech-to-text conversion, only for the duration of the transcription session.

  • AudD: When You activate the **"Recognize Music"** feature, a **short audio sample** is sent to AudD to identify the song.

3. Retention and Deletion of Your Personal Data

Retention of Your Personal Data

The Company will retain Your Personal Data, including your Detection History Metadata, only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy and to provide the Service.

Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to delete or request that We assist in deleting the Personal Data and Detection History Metadata that We have collected about You.

The Service provides You with the ability to delete Your **Detection History** through two methods:

  • Manual Deletion: You can select and delete specific sessions or choose to delete the entire history via the options available on the History screen.

  • Automatic Deletion: If configured in Settings, sessions older than 30 days will be automatically deleted from the server.

You may also contact Us to request access to, correction, or deletion of any personal information that You have provided to Us.
