2. Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

A. Types of Data Collected

Account Personal Data

We may collect the following information when You create or sign in to Your Account:

  • Email address

  • Display Name (if provided via Google sign-in)

  • Firebase User Identifier (UID)

This data is used to create and manage Your Account, authenticate You, and synchronize Your settings and history.

Detection and History Metadata

If You activate the sound detection service and are logged in, We store the following metadata in Your Detection History on Firebase Firestore:

  • Sound Labels: The specific type of sound classified by the AI (e.g., "Siren", "Fire Alarm").

  • Maximum Score: The confidence level for each detected sound.

  • Timestamps: The start time, last seen time, and creation time of the detection session.

Crucially, for the passive sound detection feature, We DO NOT record or store the raw audio data on Our servers.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically and may include Your device's IP address, device type, mobile operating system, and other diagnostic data. It is used to operate, maintain, and improve the Service.

B. Use of High-Risk Permissions (Compliance with Google Play)

Our Application requires the following sensitive permissions to deliver its core assistive functions. The use of these permissions is strictly limited and requires Your explicit consent upon first use, through a prominent in-app disclosure.

1. Microphone Access (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO)

  • Primary Use (Alarm Feature): Audio is captured continuously via a Foreground Service (which displays a persistent notification while active, including when the app is in the background) to enable Asistente Virtual IA processing (YAMNet model) on the device. The raw audio is processed instantly on the device and discarded; it is never recorded or stored by the Developer for this purpose.
  • Secondary Use (On-Demand): If You explicitly choose to use the "Start Transcription" or "Recognize Music" features, the audio is temporarily captured and transmitted to third-party providers (Deepgram and AudD) for analysis, solely to fulfill Your request.

2. Display Over Other Apps (android.permission.SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)

  • Purpose: This permission is used ONLY to display the mandatory Full-Screen Alarm Alert over the lock screen or other running applications when a critical sound is detected in the background, ensuring You receive the safety notification.

3. Camera Access (Flashlight)

  • Purpose: Access to the Camera hardware is used EXCLUSIVELY to control the device's Flashlight as a visual alarm effect, if configured by You in the Alarm Settings. We do not use this permission to record images or video.

C. Sharing of Your Personal Data with Third-Party Providers

We share Your data with the following Third-Party Providers only to enable the functionality of the Service:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore): Used for Account management, storing User Settings, and storing Detection History Metadata. See Firebase Privacy Information.

  • Deepgram: When You activate the "Start Transcription" feature, real-time audio is streamed to Deepgram for speech-to-text conversion, only for the duration of the transcription session.

  • AudD: When You activate the "Recognize Music" feature, a short audio sample is sent to AudD to identify the song.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not sell Your Personal Data, and We do not share it with third parties for advertising purposes. We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users or the Service.

3. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect Your Personal Data, including encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS) when data is sent between the Application and Our providers, authentication through Firebase, and access rules on Firestore so that Detection History is only accessible to the Account that created it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and We cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Retention and Deletion of Your Personal Data

Data Retention

We retain Your Account data and Detection History Metadata for as long as Your Account is active, or until You delete them as described below. Raw audio is not retained by Us.

Delete Your Personal Data

You have the right to delete or request that We assist in deleting the Personal Data and Detection History Metadata that We have collected about You.

The Service provides You with the ability to delete Your Detection History (partial deletion of data) through two methods:

  • Manual Deletion: You can select and delete specific sessions or choose to delete the entire history via the options available on the History screen.

  • Automatic Deletion: If configured in Settings, sessions older than 30 days will be automatically deleted from the server.

Delete Your Account

You may request the deletion of Your entire account (including all associated Personal Data and Detection History) at any time, without needing to reinstall the app, by sending an email to soporte.asistentevirtualia@gmail.com from the email address associated with Your Account, with the subject "Delete my account - Asistente Virtual IA". We will process the request and delete Your data within a reasonable period, generally within 30 days, unless We are legally required to retain certain information.

You may also contact Us to request access to or correction of any Personal Data We hold about You.

If You are a parent or guardian and believe Your child has provided Us with Personal Data, please contact Us so We can delete it.
